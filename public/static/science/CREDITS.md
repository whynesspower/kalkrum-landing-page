## Notes

- `webb-southern-ring.jpg` is cropped to the left panel (the NIRCam near-infrared view) of the side-by-side NASA image.
- `hubble-ultra-deep-field.jpg` is the Hubble eXtreme Deep Field (XDF), a deeper view of the centre of the Hubble Ultra Deep Field.
- `hubble-pillars-of-creation.jpg` and `hubble-stephans-quintet.jpg` are Hubble images. The NASA library has no Webb versions of these two subjects.
- `hubble-stephans-quintet.jpg` is cropped to remove the caption frame of the original poster and shows only part of the galaxy group.
